Custom drone

Independent project

I’m designing a quadcopter around a custom STM32 flight-controller PCB and a modular, 3D-printed frame.

The electronics include an IMU for motion sensing, regulated power, and connections to external motor controllers. The frame has removable arms with keyed joints, so the mechanical and electrical interfaces have to work together.

I’m working through component selection, assembly, and board bring-up. Flight validation is still ahead.

KiCad, Fusion 360, STM32, embedded systems.

Microbiome research

Blaser Laboratory, Rutgers · Jan–Aug 2026

As a research assistant in the Blaser Laboratory at Rutgers’ Center for Advanced Biotechnology and Medicine, I analyzed 581 human gut microbiome samples.

I studied oxalate-degrading bacteria and genetic variation linked to kidney-stone risk, building the analysis pipeline in Python on Rutgers’ Amarel supercomputing cluster.

Checking the results against controls revealed that two apparent gene variants were the same underlying sequence. A useful lesson in making sure a signal is real.

Python, metagenomics, high-performance computing, validation.
