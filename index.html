Philosophy

How I Build & Think

01

End-to-End Ownership

From schematic soldering and low-level firmware to the cloud backend and user interface, I prefer understanding the full vertical pipeline so nothing is a black box.

02

Bias Toward Rapid Working Prototypes

Theory is essential, but reality is the final validator. Getting physical hardware in the air or real code in front of actual users immediately reveals edge cases that slides miss.

03

Simplicity & Reliability Over Hype

Choose the simplest architecture that solves the problem robustly. Fewer moving parts mean lower latency, fewer failure modes, and long-term maintainability.
